Store screenshots at 1280×800

Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons screenshot size, shown here at 80%. The store shows them in order, so A is the one most people see. A and C sell the convenience; B, D and E are the reasons it is safe to enjoy.

A: “Encrypt without leaving the tab”

The lead slide, and the only one that shows the product. The panel is a real screenshot of the built extension (see apps/pgp/e2e-capture); only the header strip above it is CSS, because that strip is Chrome's, not ours.

B: “Encrypted at rest. All of it.”

The privacy slide. Shows what chrome.storage actually holds. Every store is a sealed blob, contacts and settings included, because “we encrypt your data” is what every extension says. The history row is tagged opt-in: it is off by default and most installs never create that store.
