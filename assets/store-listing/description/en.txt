Ever thought PGP is a pain to use? That's why this exists.

PGP Tools lets you encrypt, decrypt, sign, and verify messages right from your browser. Manage your keys, contacts, and operations from the side panel. No terminal required.

Your private keys are protected by passkeys (Touch ID, Face ID, YubiKey) or Argon2id passwords, and all crypto runs in Rust/WebAssembly, not in JavaScript.

Open source, free, no accounts required.

Source: https://github.com/Am-I-Being-Pwned/PGP-Tools
